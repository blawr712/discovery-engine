# v0.3 Intelligence Specification

## Objective

Improve the quality and research usefulness of Discovery Engine candidates
without sacrificing complete supported-market coverage or deterministic,
explainable scoring.

## Principles

1. Deterministic factors produce the Discovery Score.
2. AI research never changes the numerical score.
3. Every exclusion, factor, penalty, and missing value is explainable.
4. Ambiguous metadata lowers confidence; it does not silently remove a company.
5. Canadian and U.S. proportions reflect eligible listings, not quotas.
6. New scoring behavior must be comparable with the validated v0.2 baseline.

## Sprint 1: Universe Quality and Scoring Foundation

- Classify operating equities and structural vehicles from provider metadata.
- Exclude obvious SPACs, shell companies, funds, warrants, rights, units, and
  preferred securities through configuration-driven rules.
- Retain unknown classifications with an explicit confidence signal.
- Introduce a common factor-result model containing raw value, points,
  maximum points, availability, and explanation.
- Add score confidence and a machine-readable factor breakdown to reports.
- Preserve v0.2 score values while the framework is introduced.
- Collect provider fundamentals through a versioned metadata schema.
- Calculate growth, profitability, cash-flow, and balance-sheet shadow factors
  before integrating them into the Discovery Score.
- Export curated candidates and factor-coverage analytics.
- Support deterministic balanced validation samples across Canada and the U.S.

## Planned Sprint 2: Financial Intelligence

- Add growth, profitability, free-cash-flow, balance-sheet, valuation, and
  risk factors.
- Define missing-data and stale-data policies for every factor.
- Rebalance weights only after distribution and candidate-review analysis.

### Increment 1: Valuation, Risk, and Data Quality

- Add shadow earnings-yield, sales-yield, and enterprise-value-to-EBITDA
  valuation factors.
- Add shadow liquidity, leverage, and cash-flow-to-earnings quality factors.
- Classify factor inputs as fresh, undated, missing, invalid, or stale.
- Exclude stale and invalid values from scoring rather than treating them as
  weak financial performance.
- Discount confidence for usable but undated provider fundamentals.
- Keep the official Discovery Score and its ranking unchanged.

### Increment 2: Validation and Calibration

- Export technical, country-relative, sector-relative, and fundamental
  percentiles for every successful candidate.
- Measure technical/fundamental rank correlation and top-candidate overlap.
- Surface the largest rank disagreements, low-confidence candidates, factor
  distributions, and implausible provider-value outliers.
- Calculate configurable experimental blend scenarios using comparable
  percentile ranks.
- Preserve official Discovery Score order in every calibration export and
  prevent experimental scenarios from controlling candidate ranking.

### Increment 3: Calibration Hardening

- Apply configurable `cap` or `invalid` treatment to extreme provider inputs
  while preserving the original source value for audit.
- Mark sector-inappropriate factors as not applicable and remove their weight
  from both normalized scoring and confidence calculations.
- Confidence-adjust fundamental scores and require minimum confidence before a
  candidate can enter a blended experimental scenario.
- Add sector-relative fundamental percentiles and explicit blend-ineligibility
  explanations.
- Report top-20, top-50, and top-100 overlap plus scenario promotions and
  demotions.
- Classify factor readiness as `ready`, `limited`, or `shadow_only` from
  applicable-data coverage.
- Keep all hardened fundamentals and blends in the non-authoritative shadow
  lane.

### Increment 4: Coverage-Neutral Blending and Acceptance Gates

- Select a core fundamental lane dynamically from factors meeting the minimum
  coverage threshold in both Canada and the United States.
- Calculate country-relative or country/sector-relative fundamental
  percentiles, falling back to the country peer group when a sector sample is
  too small.
- Scale low-confidence peer percentiles continuously toward neutral instead of
  applying a hard eligibility cutoff.
- Let technical scoring define a fixed top-100 candidate pool and allow
  fundamentals to rerank only within 25-company technical bands.
- Explain candidate-pool membership separately from fundamental-data
  eligibility.
- Measure country eligibility disparity, top-list retention, and maximum rank
  movement against configurable acceptance gates.
- Emit an automatic pass/fail decision with reasons for every experimental
  scenario.

## Planned Sprint 3: Candidate Reporting

- Export a ranked research-candidate report separately from diagnostic rows.
- Include rank, percentile, strongest signals, risks, factor coverage, and
  score explanations.

### Increment 1: Decision-Ready Scenario Reports

- Rebuild calibration and research artifacts from a completed run without
  initializing market-data providers or downloading data.
- Validate saved run identifiers, completion state, and checkpoint
  completeness before recalibration.
- Export one experimental research queue for each passing weighted scenario.
- Include official and experimental rank, movement, technical score, core
  fundamentals, confidence, peer percentile, and peer group.
- Explain promotions, demotions, core strengths, core weaknesses, missing or
  treated data, and outlier flags.
- Export a combined top-25-per-scenario human-review queue and scenario
  country/sector composition summary.
- Record recalibration provenance and generated artifacts in the source run
  manifest.

## Planned Sprint 4: AI Research

- Generate optional, cached, source-backed summaries only for top deterministic
  candidates.
- Cover business overview, growth drivers, risks, recent developments, and
  unanswered research questions.

## v0.3 Acceptance Criteria

- Structural false positives are materially reduced in the top 100.
- Scores remain deterministic and fully decomposable into factors.
- Missing data is visible through factor availability and score confidence.
- A full-universe run completes with zero pipeline errors.
- AI output is optional and cannot alter ranking.
